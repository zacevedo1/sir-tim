Sir Tim Berners-Lee

Sir Timothy John Berners-Lee, OM, KBE, FRS, FREng, FRSA (born 8 June 1955) is an English engineer and computer scientist, best known as the inventor of the World Wide Web. He is a professor at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) and the University of Oxford. He is also a director of the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), which oversees the development of web standards.
